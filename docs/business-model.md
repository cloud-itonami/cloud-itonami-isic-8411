# Business Model: Community Public Administration Service

## Classification
- Repository: `cloud-itonami-8411`
- ISIC Rev.5: `8411` — public administration — case intake, decision, notification and appeal for community services
- Social impact: civic-access transparency accountability

## Customer
- municipalities and community programs that cannot accept closed government-services SaaS

## Offer
- case intake, decision workflows, notification and appeal records, public disclosure and audit

## Revenue
- setup fee per program, monthly operations subscription, integration services

## Trust Controls
- decisions outside authority are blocked; notifications are auditable; appeals are mandatory; citizen data stays outside Git
- a robot action the governor refuses is never dispatched to hardware
- every dispatch, hold, approval and disclosure path is auditable
- sensitive operating and personal data stays outside Git
